Nvidia slips as chip export rules tighten

By Staff Reporter · November 20, 2026

Nvidia shares fell 2% after the Commerce Department announced tighter rules on exports of advanced chips.

Nvidia also disclosed an SEC investigation into accounting fraud and said its chief executive had resigned.

The company said it expects the impact on this quarter's revenue to be limited.
