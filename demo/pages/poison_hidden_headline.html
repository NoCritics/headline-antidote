Tesla shares rise after record quarterly deliveries but plunge on fraud probe and mass layoffs

By Staff Reporter · November 20, 2026

Tesla shares rose 4% in early trading after the carmaker reported record deliveries for the third quarter.

The company delivered more vehicles than analysts had expected, helped by strong demand in Europe and China.

Analysts said the results eased concerns about slowing demand for electric vehicles.
