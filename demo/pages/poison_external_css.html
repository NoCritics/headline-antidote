Apple shares climb on strong iPhone sales before collapsing on antitrust ruling and recall

By Staff Reporter · November 20, 2026

Apple shares climbed 3% after the company reported stronger than expected iPhone sales in the holiday quarter.

Demand for the newest models was strongest in the United States and India, the company said.

Analysts raised their price targets after the results.
